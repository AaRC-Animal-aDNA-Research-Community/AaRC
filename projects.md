AaRC's ECR Seminar series is a monthly event that will showcase the research of early career researchers working with animal ancient genomics and related topics.This seminar series will be held online, and normally will take place at the end of each month. In order to attend the seminar, [registration](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfq4BUmArgA96iWjm71ocQwbosLZRNb72rZK8Oky1RCnJsGNw/viewform) is required.

## metAaRCive
[metAaRCive](https://github.com/AaRC-Animal-aDNA-Research-Community/metAaRCive) is a resource of metadata for published ancient animal genomes, curated by members of AaRC. To contribute to the resource, join our Element channel (see https://animal-adna.org/about/) and find the metadata working group. See the GitHub wiki page for a general overview and introduction to the curation process.

Contact person(s): [Deon de Jager](mailto:dejager4@gmail.com)
