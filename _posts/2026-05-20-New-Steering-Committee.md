With AaRC making two years on April 2026, the funding members of the Steering Committee stepped down from their positions and let the new members take charge. 
Pedro Morell Miranda will step down as chair, but remain as webmaster. 
After some delay on our side, [the new composition of the Steering Committee is on the website](https://animal-adna.org/steering_committee/), along with a new section for former members. 

We would like to thank all former members for their work and great contributions on making AaRC what it is today.
